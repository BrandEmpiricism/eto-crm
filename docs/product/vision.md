# Product vision

## Target customer

Small and medium engineered-to-order manufacturers that sell custom tools, fixtures, machines, fabricated products, automation, or engineering solutions.

## Problem

Prospective customers are often identified inconsistently, marketing describes products instead of customer outcomes, and context is lost as work passes from business development to sales and engineering. Generic CRMs track contacts and pipeline stages but do not ensure that technical opportunities are ready for engineering.

## Promise

Find the right manufacturer, identify a problem we can solve, demonstrate the relevant capability, qualify the need, and hand complete information to engineering.

## Product principles

1. Guide the next useful action.
2. Use manufacturing language.
3. Ask only for information needed at the current stage.
4. Make ownership and missing information visible.
5. Provide strong defaults before configuration.
6. Configure predictable variation; code business-specific behavior.
7. Automation follows a validated manual workflow.

## First outcome

A business-development user can create a prospective account, record an observed need or signal, match it to a company capability, and schedule a focused next action.
